# Practice Tools

**Improve your practice sessions with useful real-time statistics!**

Practice Tools is a lightweight Geometry Dash mod that displays helpful information while playing in Practice Mode.

## Features

- **Practice HUD** — View your practice statistics directly on the screen.
- **Attempt Counter** — Track your attempts during a practice session.
- **Checkpoint Counter** — See how many active checkpoints you have.
- **Progress Tracker** — Display your current and best practice progress.
- **Customizable Settings** — Enable or disable individual HUD features.
- **Practice Only** — The HUD automatically hides in Normal Mode.

## Compatibility

- Geometry Dash 2.2091
- Geode 6.0.0-alpha.2
- Windows
